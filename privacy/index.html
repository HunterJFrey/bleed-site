Bleed privacy policy

Effective [DATE]. Bleed is made by [YOUR NAME].

Bleed is an ink puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: no accounts, no names, no email addresses, no location. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics so the puzzles can be tuned, and you can turn that off.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, settings, solved puzzles and gallery are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game sends: anonymous gameplay statistics

To find puzzles that are too hard or too easy, Bleed sends anonymous events to PostHog, an analytics service that processes the data on our behalf. The events describe how the game is played, for example:

  • which puzzle was opened, how many attempts it took, how long it took, and whether a hint or step mode was used;
  • when a world is unlocked, and when the game is opened or closed;
  • basic technical details: app version, device model, operating system version and language.

Events are grouped by a random identifier created when the game is first installed. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, advertising ID or any account. IP addresses are discarded and are not used to work out your location. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

We use this information only to understand how the game is played and to improve it. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone other than PostHog, which processes it for us.

Turning it off

Open Settings in the game and switch off Share anonymous usage data. The game then stops sending events.

Sharing your artwork

When you choose to share a solved puzzle from the gallery, the image is passed to your phone's own share menu. Bleed does not receive or keep a copy of where you send it.

Ads and purchases

Test versions of Bleed do not show real ads or sell anything. Before ads or the Remove Ads purchase are added, this policy will be updated to describe the ad and payment providers and what they collect.

Children

Bleed is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Because the statistics are anonymous and not linked to you, we have no way to find one player's events. Switching off Share anonymous usage data stops any more from being sent, and uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your phone. Questions are welcome at the address below.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
